Questions

Is there real AI in it?

No. It's a game about the hype, not a product of it. The tokens are glowing hexagons, and the agents only wear sunglasses.

What is that thing crossing the road?

An LLM. The first one is 1B parameters. Every one after it is bigger, by a random factor, until they're measured in trillions.

What do I spend tokens on?

The locker: nine outfits for the runner and the vibe laptop. Two can't be bought; they come with the Unicorn and Legend titles. And once a run, a git revert.

Is there a daily challenge?

The Daily Sprint: the same course for everyone today, with your best and a day streak.

Is there music?

A song for each zone, made in the game itself: drums and bass, an arp when you hit a Flow streak, a lead when a power-up kicks in. It has its own switch in the settings.

How does a run end?

A first hit is a stumble: your Flow resets and the blimp closes in. A second hit within six seconds and prod is down. Once a run, banked tokens buy a git revert.

What are missions for?

Three at a time, from “Collect 50 tokens” to “Reach The Cloud”. Each one banks tokens and raises your title, from Intern to Legend.

Does it collect anything?

No. Your best score and your token bank stay on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere. It plays offline.
