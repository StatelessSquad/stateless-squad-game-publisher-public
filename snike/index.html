Questions

How do I steer?

Drag anywhere: a stick appears under your thumb and the head turns toward it. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD.

What does ⚡ do?

A dash: a burst of speed that recharges in about two seconds. Space does it on a keyboard. Mid-dash, your head bites shades and crunches beetles. It won't save you from a hawk: get off its line.

How many levels are there?

120. Each asks for a few more meals, and every fifth one is a 15-second bonus round. A new run starts at the level you reached.

When do I evolve?

A newt with hind legs at level 5, a lizard at 11, a basilisk with a crest at 31 and a dragon with wings at 61. Between levels you puff up for a second, then shed your skin.

What ends a run?

A wall, your own body, a shade, a beetle or a hawk. A bonus round can't: its walls bounce. The game over screen says what it was, and you try that level again.

Is there an ending?

Yes: clear level 120 and you're the Dragon of the dark, with a crown on the title screen. Then Endless mode keeps going, a little faster every round.

Is there a dark mode?

It follows your phone: a neon night or a sunlit meadow. You can also choose Light or Dark in the settings.

Does it collect anything?

No. Your best score, level, longest run and crown stay on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere. It plays offline.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases and no account to make.
