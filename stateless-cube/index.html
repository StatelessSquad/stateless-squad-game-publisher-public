Questions

How do I play?

Swipe, or use the arrow keys. Matching icons merge into the next one, and a new icon appears after every move. Build the StateLess-Cube (the 2048 tile) to win, then keep going.

What is a combo?

Merge again within about a second and a half and the combo climbs: ×2, ×3 and up. Combos charge the booster meter faster.

How do I get boosters?

Every merge charges the meter under the score. A full meter earns a hammer, a shuffle or ×2; tap one in the dock, or press 1, 2 or 3.

Can I take a move back?

Yes: undo takes back your last move.

Does it need the internet?

No. It plays offline, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your best score and level stay on your device.
