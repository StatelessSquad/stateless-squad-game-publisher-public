Questions

Is it free?

Yes. BuildingByTouching is free to play, with no ads and no accounts.

Do I need the network to play?

No. The game is playable offline; only the stores and the site are online.

What platforms is it on?

The web, iOS and Android, from one build.

How do I play?

One tap. The card at the bottom left shows what you are building and the glowing pad shows where the next piece goes. The crane swings it over; tap the moment it is right above the mark.

What is the stability meter?

How soundly the building is going up: the share of your lifts that ended up bearing properly on what is under them. Above 70% means it really was built properly.
