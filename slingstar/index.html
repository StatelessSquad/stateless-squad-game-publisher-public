Questions

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to let the comet go. It flies toward the next planet; land near the centre for a perfect catch.

What happens when I miss?

You lose a life and an eighth of your score, and the comet returns to its orbit. Clearing a galaxy gives a life back.

Does anything cost money?

No. Stardust comes only from playing, and the Hangar is cosmetic.
