Privacy policy · updated 2026-10-29

Your privacy in STM Defender

STM Defender does not collect any personal data. Not your name, not your email, not your location, not who you are.

What the game stores

Your progress and settings — scores, levels, streaks, sound and appearance — are saved on your device only, in the app's own storage. They never leave your device and we cannot see them. Deleting the app, or tapping Reset progress in the game's settings, erases them.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no profiles.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No network connection is needed to play; the game does not send anything anywhere.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

The stores

Apple's App Store and Google Play may collect data about downloads and crashes under their own privacy policies. You can manage that in your device settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it and the new policy is published here. Questions? open a request.
