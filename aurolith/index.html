Questions

How do I play?

Tap anywhere, or press Space, to drop the sliding slab onto the tower. Whatever hangs over the edge is sliced away, so the next slab is that much smaller.

How do I keep the slab whole?

Drop it dead on the slab below. Three perfect drops in a row start to grow it back; six charge a Prism that comes back at full size.

What ends a run?

A drop that misses the tower completely. A slice only makes the next slab smaller. In a Bonus Rush a miss is forgiven: the Rush ends and the run goes on.

How high does the tower go?

As high as you can stack it. Dawn is the start, the Cloud Sea begins at slab 20, the Aurora at 45, Orbit at 75 and the Nebula at 110. Each world slides a little faster and turns the tower the other way.

Does it collect anything?

No. Your best score stays on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere. It plays offline.
