Questions

What age is it for?

Three to seven. Grown-ups pick the age in the settings: little ones (3–4) listen and find pictures; big ones (5–7) also match and spell.

How does it decide what to practice?

Spaced repetition: a word comes back after one day, then two, four, a week, two weeks. A missed word comes back the same day. New words arrive two at a time.

Does it collect anything?

No. Progress stays on the device, the voice is the device's own, and nothing is sent anywhere. Links out of the app are behind a parental gate.
